Smart Strike vs Sniper — what is the difference

Smart Strike is an aggressive algorithmic strategy. The key change: every trade now has a stop-loss. Risk is limited by the design of the system, not by a promise.

  • Return: up to 50%+ per month, depending on market conditions.
  • Risk: drawdowns can be substantial and are measured from the current balance, not from the initial deposit. The strategy is not for the faint-hearted.
  • Key feature: a stop-loss on every trade — the account is not liquidated and positions are not force-closed; after a drawdown the system recovers and keeps working.

Dear clients, please understand this in advance: SmartStrike is an aggressive strategy. It does have a stop-loss on every trade, but three stop-losses in a row — or more — can noticeably reduce your main deposit. That is part of how the system works, not a failure. So please do not fund SmartStrike with a large deposit: it is better to grow the account gradually and withdraw part of the profit regularly. For large deposits we recommend the Sniper trading system.

Sniper — positional trading with a strict stop-loss on every trade.

The system is maximally cautious: only a few trades a month, so a week — or even two — can pass with no trades at all. That is normal operation, not a standstill. Entry selection is strict: better to skip a trade than to take a weak one.

  • Return: from 10% to 30%+ per month, depending on market conditions.
  • Risk: moderate — the loss on a single trade is limited in advance.
  • Best for: larger amounts and a calm pace; the result shows over six months or more.

Bottom line: Smart Strike offers higher potential returns, but it takes patience and a willingness to sit through a substantial drawdown without touching the account. Sniper is less stressful with a smoother result. The choice depends on your money management and temperament; using both systems together gives maximum diversification.
